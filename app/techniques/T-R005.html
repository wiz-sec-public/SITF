SITF technique T-R005: Publishing Malicious Container/VM Image

Component: RegistryStage: Discovery and Lateral MovementID: T-R005

Attacker publishes malicious container or VM image.

SITF technique T-R005 (Publishing Malicious Container/VM Image) is a Discovery and Lateral Movement technique in the SDLC Infrastructure Threat Framework (SITF). It targets the Registry component (Container and artifact registries (NPM, Docker, PyPI)).

Risks that enable this technique

  • Overprivileged registry credentials
  • No image signing
  • No provenance attestation
  • No image scanning
  • Provenance not verified before publishing

Protective controls

  • Trusted publishing
    OWASP SPVS: V3.2 Credential Hygiene
  • Provenance attestation
    OWASP SPVS: V3.4 Integrity of Artifacts
  • Image signing and verification
    OWASP SPVS: V3.4 Integrity of Artifacts
  • OIDC for publishing
    OWASP SPVS: V1.1 Identity and Access Management; V3.2 Credential Hygiene
  • Pre-publish provenance verification
    OWASP SPVS: V3.4 Integrity of Artifacts

Detective controls

  • Image vulnerability scanning
    OWASP SPVS: V5.4 Detection & Monitoring
